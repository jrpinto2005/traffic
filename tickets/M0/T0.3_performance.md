# T0.3 — Performance baseline: transport and query strategy

**Archetype:** Spike
**Milestone:** M0
**Depends on:** T0.2
**Implements decisions:** confirms `D-002`, `D-031`

## Context
`D-002` assumes LibSUMO is roughly 10× faster than TraCI, and `D-031` makes
subscription-based reads an **architectural constraint** rather than an optimization, on the
grounds that round-trips dominate. Both are published claims. Neither has been measured for
this project's query pattern, and `D-031` requires that "slow" be a measurement rather than
an impression.

## Questions
1. TraCI vs LibSUMO wall-clock on the T0.2 scenario, under a realistic per-step query load.
2. Variable/context subscriptions vs naive per-object polling, same scenario, same data.
3. What is the baseline **wall-clock per simulated hour** that later milestones compare
   against?
4. Does LibSUMO behave as documented — one simulation per process, no GUI?

## Method
Read, every step, what M1 will actually need: per-vehicle position, speed, type, `timeLoss`,
`waitingTime`; per-lane halting count and occupancy; per-intersection signal state. Measure
four combinations (TraCI/LibSUMO × subscriptions/polling), each repeated enough times to
report a spread rather than a single number.

## Deliverable
`research/M0_performance.md` with a four-cell table of wall-clock per simulated hour, the
measurement method, the hardware, and a statement of whether `D-002` and `D-031` are
confirmed or need amendment.

## Non-goals
1. Do NOT optimize anything. Measure only. `D-031`.
2. Do NOT build the transport abstraction. That is T1.4.
3. Do NOT test at network sizes beyond T0.2's single intersection.

## Escalate if
1. The LibSUMO speedup is under ~2× — `D-002` stays (the abstraction is nearly free) but its
   priority changes, which is a decision-level finding.
2. LibSUMO is unusable for a reason not anticipated in `D-002`.
