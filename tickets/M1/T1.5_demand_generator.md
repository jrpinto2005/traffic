# T1.5 — Demand: sources, turn ratios, attrition, sinks

**Archetype:** Builder · **Depends on:** T1.3, T0.5 · **Implements:** `D-022`, `D-017`

## Context
The owner-specified open-boundary flow model: vehicles are **not conserved** inside the study
area. They enter from Poisson sources, propagate by turning ratios, and leave either at a
boundary sink or **mid-link**, having reached a local destination.

Demand design determines what an experiment can show, so getting this subtly wrong is
expensive and invisible.

## Contract
Implement `DemandSpec` and its components exactly as `05_DOMAIN_MODEL.md` §6.1 specifies.

```python
def build_routes(net: Network, spec: DemandSpec, seed: int, out: Path) -> Path:
    """Emit SUMO route/flow files. Returns the path passed to Backend.start()."""
```

**Use the modern turn-file format.** SUMO 1.27.1 deprecates `<fromEdge>` / `<toEdge>`; emit
`<edgeRelation from=... to=... probability=.../>` instead. Tutorials and older examples show the
legacy form, so copying one means shipping deprecated XML from the first commit.

Realisation, per `D-022` and confirmed by T0.5: `<flow>` with exponential headways for
Poisson arrivals; `jtrrouter` for turn-ratio routing with declared sink edges; `departPos`
for mid-link sources; `arrivalPos` for mid-link attrition.

**Rates are a fraction of the receiving approach's saturation flow**, not absolute veh/h, so
"demand level 0.8" means the same loading across geometries. Saturation flow is computed from
lane count and speed limit; document the formula used.

Seeded from the `demand` stream only (`D-017`).

## Scope
1. `DemandSpec` → SUMO route files.
2. Saturation-flow computation and the fraction-to-absolute conversion.
3. Per-edge attrition applied at route construction: locally-destined vehicles get routes
   terminating at a sampled `arrivalPos` on that edge.
4. Vehicle type mix (cars only at M1, but the parameter exists so M4 needs no schema change).
5. Log the count of route generations that hit `jtrrouter`'s maximum-edges limit — the
   memoryless-routing artifact `D-022` warns about.
6. **Invariants 15–16** of `05_DOMAIN_MODEL.md` §8, moved here from T1.2 because §6's
   `TurnRatioSpec` and `AttritionSpec` are built in this ticket: turn ratios sum to 1.0 per
   (junction, from_edge) within tolerance, and attrition rates lie in [0, 1). Both are load-time
   validation errors naming the offending edge, not runtime surprises.

## Non-goals
1. Do NOT implement time-varying demand. That is M8.
2. Do NOT implement route choice or re-routing. Routes are fixed at insertion — deliberate,
   because route choice would make control effects unattributable.
3. Do NOT implement OD matrices. Turning ratios only.
4. Do NOT make attrition a single global rate. Per-edge, per `D-022`.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Arrival headways are statistically consistent with the configured Poisson rate
      (Kolmogorov–Smirnov against the exponential, on ≥ 10,000 arrivals).
- [ ] The same seed produces byte-identical route files. Different seeds do not.
- [ ] Configured attrition rate matches the realised fraction of mid-link arrivals within
      tolerance, verified against `tripinfo`.
- [ ] Turn ratios per (junction, from-edge) sum to 1.0 and the realised split matches within
      tolerance.
- [ ] Demand level 0.8 produces comparable loading on a 2-lane and a 3-lane approach.
- [ ] The max-edges-limit counter is logged even when zero.

## Escalate if
1. T0.5 found that mid-link arrivals are not distinguishable in `tripinfo` — the three-way
   accounting in `07_EXPERIMENT_METHODOLOGY.md` §4 depends on it.
2. `jtrrouter` cannot express per-edge attrition and an alternative realisation is needed.
3. The saturation-flow formula requires a judgement call not fixed by `D-022`.

## Amendment, 2026-10-07 — binding; issued with the ticket

Written after T1.3, T1.4 and T1.14 merged and after `D-037`. Where this section and anything
above disagree, **this section wins**.

### A1. Saturation flow is calibrated in SUMO (`D-037`) — Escalate-if 3 is answered
- `calibrate_saturation_flow(...)` measures per-lane saturation flow for a (vehicle type,
  speed limit, turn direction): that movement alone on a T1.3-generated intersection, held
  green for the whole run, a saturated queue, arrivals counted over a steady window after the
  queue forms, at least five seeds; report mean and spread. Reference measurement by the
  architect (one seed, default passenger, 13.89 m/s): through 1652, left 1624, right 1392
  veh/h/lane — yours should land near these.
- Store results in a committed JSON under `configs/`, keyed by SUMO version and a hash of the
  `vType` parameters; a lookup against a different SUMO version or `vType` **raises** rather
  than reusing stale numbers.
- An approach's saturation flow = its lane count × the per-lane through value. A source's
  absolute rate = `rate_saturation_fraction` × that.
- Calibration runs SUMO through the `sumo` binary (`sumolib.checkBinary`) — `scenarios/` may
  use `sumolib` but never `traci` or `libsumo` (`06_ARCHITECTURE.md` §10).

### A2. Signature
`build_routes(net_file: Path, network: Network, ids: SumoIds, spec: DemandSpec, seed: int,
out: Path) -> Path`. `jtrrouter` needs the net file and SUMO edge IDs, which the original
signature did not carry. `seed` is the `demand` stream's seed (`D-017`); pass it to `jtrrouter`
too.

### A3. Realisation details
- `jtrrouter` expands flows into explicit `<vehicle>` elements (verified 2026-10-07). Apply
  attrition by post-processing its routes: walk each route, and on an edge with attrition rate
  `a` truncate there with probability `a` and a sampled `arrivalPos`, using a generator seeded
  from the demand stream. Same seed → byte-identical files.
- **Attrition at M1.** A single intersection has no interior edges; attrition may be declared
  on approach edges (the vehicle leaves before the stop line) and exit edges. Test both.
- The type mix writes one `<vType>` per `VehicleType` with its `vClass` (`passenger`,
  `motorcycle`), so T1.8's tripinfo reader can resolve types.

### A4. Acceptance criteria, amended
- The Kolmogorov–Smirnov test runs on **scheduled** departures in the route file.
- Replace "Demand level 0.8 produces comparable loading" with: **the same demand level gives
  the same realised flow per unit of saturation flow on a 2-lane and a 3-lane approach**,
  within tolerance, at a level the protected four-phase plan can serve (≤ 0.15). Level 0.8 is
  several times the capacity of any signalised approach.
- Add: calibration is reproducible — two calibrations with the same seeds give the same
  numbers — and a lookup with a mismatched SUMO version raises.
