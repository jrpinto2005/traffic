# Ticket T3.5: Sensing-Control Gap Evaluation and Attribution Report

- **Milestone:** M3 (Imperfect observation and sensing–control gap)
- **Status:** Complete
- **Decision References:** `D-017` (paired Monte Carlo), `D-018` (windows), `D-021` (decay estimator), `03_ROADMAP.md` M3 exit criteria.

## 1. Goal

Run the comprehensive sensing-control gap experimental suite across all 4 controllers
(Fixed-Time, Actuated, Webster, Max-Pressure) under varying sensing quality regimes and camera
placements. Produce the headline degradation figure and attribution analysis report.

## 2. Experimental Design

1. **Sensing Regimes:**
   - **Oracle Arm:** Perfect ground truth (`OracleSensor` + `IdentityEstimator`).
   - **Ideal Camera Arm:** 100% detection within 4-approach camera frustums (`latency=0, noise=0`).
   - **Degraded Camera Sweep:**
     - Detection probability $P_{\text{det}} \in \{0.5, 0.7, 0.85, 0.95\}$.
     - Position noise $\sigma_{xy} \in \{0.0, 1.0, 2.5, 5.0\}\text{ m}$.
     - Transmission latency $\tau \in \{0.0, 1.0, 3.0, 5.0\}\text{ s}$.
2. **Sensor Placement Variations:**
   - **Config 1 (Full 4-Approach):** 1 camera mounted at stop line of each approach pointing upstream.
   - **Config 2 (Single Central):** 1 360-degree omni camera at intersection center with $R=80\text{ m}$.
   - **Config 3 (Partial 2-Approach):** Cameras on East-West approaches only; North-South estimated purely by decay.
3. **Attribution Analysis (§23):**
   - Attribute performance loss $\Delta J = J_{\text{degraded}} - J_{\text{oracle}}$ to:
     - **Sensing error** (unobserved arrivals, dropouts)
     - **Estimation error** (decay lag, unassigned lanes)
     - **Control fragility** (controllers unable to handle delayed or uncertain inputs)
